# Project Plan

Amaravati Rising is a realistic city-building tycoon game focused on developing the planned smart capital of Andhra Pradesh along the Krishna River. It emphasizes sustainability, smart infrastructure, green spaces, riverfront development, and Indian cultural elements. Core gameplay involves building, managing resources (Money, Population, Happiness, Water, Power, Pollution), and achieving sustainability targets. Key features include Krishna River water management, smart city ICT, solar energy, EV transport, and realistic monsoon effects. Target audience: Casual and mid-core players. Platform: Android. UI includes a main menu, in-game city view with resource bars, building selection menu, and a management dashboard.

## Project Brief

# Project Brief: Amaravati Rising

Amaravati Rising is a realistic city-building tycoon game for Android, challenging players to develop the sustainable, smart capital of Andhra Pradesh.
 Players must balance rapid urban growth with ecological preservation along the Krishna River, integrating modern ICT infrastructure with Indian cultural heritage.


### Features
*   **Interactive City Construction**: A grid-based building system for placing smart residential, commercial, and government zones
, including iconic landmarks and green spaces.
*   **Sustainability & Resource Management**: A real-time dashboard to monitor and optimize
 Money, Population, Happiness, Water, Power, and Pollution levels to reach green city targets.
*   **Riverfront
 & Eco-System Development**: Specialized mechanics for managing the Krishna River waterfront, implementing solar farms, and maintaining the ecological balance.

*   **Smart Infrastructure & EV Transport**: Integration of advanced ICT networks and Electric Vehicle (EV) transport systems to enhance
 city efficiency and reduce the carbon footprint.

### High-Level Technical Stack
*   **Language**: Kotlin
*   **
UI Framework**: Jetpack Compose with Material Design 3
*   **Navigation**: Jetpack Navigation 3 (State-driven)

*   **Adaptive Strategy**: Compose Material Adaptive library for seamless support across handsets, foldables, and tablets
*   **
Concurrency**: Kotlin Coroutines
